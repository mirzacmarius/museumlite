A finely carved marble bust depicting the Emperor Marcus Aurelius in his later years. The emperor is shown wearing a paludamentum (military cloak) fastened at the right shoulder with a fibula. His beard is long and philosopher-like, hair deeply drilled to create contrast with the smooth skin of the face. The eyes are heavy-lidded, gazing slightly upward and to the right, suggesting a contemplative demeanor typical of Antonine portraiture.
