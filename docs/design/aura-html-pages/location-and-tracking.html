Curatorial Statement

"Ancient Metals" explores the transformative power of metallurgy in the Bronze and Iron Ages. From the ritualistic use of gold in funerary practices to the utilitarian revolution of iron weaponry, this exhibition traces the technological and cultural shifts that defined early civilizations.
