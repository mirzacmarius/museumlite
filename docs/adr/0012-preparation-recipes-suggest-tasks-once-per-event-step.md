# Preparation recipes suggest case tasks once per case event and step

An accepted intake (an approved accession), an approved loan and an approved exhibition are case events that preparation recipes follow. Without an approval workflow an intake is accepted when it is created or submitted, and an exhibition when it is created; its creator is then the member who accepted it. A recipe is institution configuration: its trigger, for loans optionally a direction, and up to ten steps, each with a title, an owner rule, a due date in days from the acceptance or the case's start or end, the earlier steps it waits for, and optionally the kind of evidence that shows it was done. Recipes are opt-in. None exist until an administrator creates one, so releasing the feature changes nothing for an organization that has not configured it.

Suggestions are made inside the transaction that accepts the case, by `suggestPreparation` (`packages/backend/convex/utils/preparation.ts`), so a case and its suggestions commit together. Each suggestion is a case task (MUS-656) whose `suggestionKey` names the event, the recipe and the step. The task table holds at most one task per key, whatever its status, and the owner's `task_assigned` notification is dispatched under the same key (ADR 0007). Repeating the event (a workflow or bulk approval reaching its final step, or staff applying the recipes again) only adds steps that have no task yet. Correcting the event's dates (an approved loan extension, a changed exhibition opening or closing) moves the due date of open suggestions whose date staff have not changed, and adds nothing. A dismissed suggestion, which is a cancelled task, is never recreated, and staff can restore it. Reversing the event, which today means cancelling an approved loan before dispatch, withdraws the suggestions nobody has started; started work stays for staff to close.

The owner is the step's named member, else the member assigned to the case, else the member who accepted it: the first of them who can work on the case. A step without such an owner is skipped, and the case shows it as waiting for an owner until someone applies the recipes again. A step that names evidence cannot be completed or submitted for review until that kind of evidence is linked to its task. Suggestions never record custody, receipt, installation, title or condition: completing one changes only the task, and the physical record is made through its own guarded command, which the linked evidence cites.

Considered and rejected: a separate queue of suggestions that staff accept before they become tasks, because it would split My work and leave work without an owner while nobody reviews it; scheduling the suggestions after the acceptance, because that needs its own retry ledger and lets a case and its preparation disagree after a failure; and completing a task automatically when matching evidence appears, because a record link would then state that the work was done.
