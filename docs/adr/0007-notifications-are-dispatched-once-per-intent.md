# Notifications are dispatched once per intent under each recipient's channel policy

Every notification starts as one notification intent, recorded by `dispatchNotification` (`packages/backend/convex/utils/notificationDispatch.ts`) inside the transaction of the change that caused it. The intent captures the type, its category, the title, message and related entity as they were at that moment, optional email details, and an optional idempotency key. Dispatching again with the same organization and key returns the first intent and writes nothing, so a replayed job or a retried command notifies once. Emitters never insert notifications or schedule emails themselves.

For each recipient the dispatcher requires a current membership of the organization and, when the notification names an entity, the role permission that reads that entity type (for example `loans.read` for loans, `collections.read` for accessions); a recipient who fails either check gets nothing. It then applies the recipient's policy for the type's category. A disabled category gets nothing on any channel. An enabled category always gets the in-app notification, so the notification center is the complete trace of what a recipient was sent. Email is an additional channel per category: it is added when the recipient turned email on for that category, the account has an address and the deployment has an email transport. Recipients without saved choices get every category in the app and no email. Choices saved by the previous settings page are read as they were offered: instant email covered approvals and tasks.

Instant email is an at-least-once delivery on the ledger of ADR 0004, with the same statuses, attempts, backoff, lease, sweep, 30-day retention and recipient-started retry series. Each attempt first rechecks membership, content access, the category's email choice and the address, and cancels instead of sending when one no longer holds. The provider receives `{delivery id}:{series}` as its idempotency key, so an attempt that is resent after its result was lost is not delivered twice within the provider's 24-hour window; a retry series uses a new key. Only the recipient can retry their email. Without `RESEND_API_KEY` (or the loopback-only `NOTIFICATION_EMAIL_FIXTURE_URL` used by tests and local QA) the email channel is reported as unavailable and is not offered.

Daily and weekly digests are a cadence of the email channel. They are not offered until a digest worker exists; a stored digest choice sends nothing and the settings page says so. A digest worker plugs into the dispatcher's email cadence decision instead of adding a second dispatcher.

Considered and rejected: per-recipient rows without an intent, because idempotency, digests and alert episodes need one record per logical event; and keeping the separate notification helpers, because each had diverged in preference handling and none validated recipients.
