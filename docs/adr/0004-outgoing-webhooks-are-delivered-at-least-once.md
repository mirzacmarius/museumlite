# Outgoing webhooks are delivered at least once from a captured snapshot

An emitted event is stored with a versioned payload (version 1: `id`, `event`, `version`, `timestamp`, `data`) in the same transaction as the change that caused it, together with one delivery per subscribed active endpoint. Each delivery is attempted up to 6 times with jittered backoff (30s, 2m, 8m, 32m, 2h); 2xx succeeds, 408, 429, 5xx, timeouts and network errors are retried, and other responses fail permanently. A disabled, deleted or unsubscribed endpoint cancels the delivery without sending. An administrator retry starts a new attempt series on the same delivery, so the body and `X-ML-Delivery` id never change and receivers deduplicate on that id; we accept duplicate sends over lost ones.

`X-ML-Signature` stays `sha256=` HMAC-SHA256 of the body so existing receivers keep verifying. `X-ML-Signature-V2` is `v2=` HMAC-SHA256 of `{X-ML-Delivery}.{X-ML-Timestamp}.{body}` for receivers that want replay protection. Both use the endpoint's current secret at send time. Only events with a wired emit point are advertised. Delivery logs are operational telemetry rather than institutional records (ADR 0002 does not apply) and are purged after 30 days.
