# Institutional records are permanent; migrations are additive

Existing records, identifiers and accession history are never deleted or renumbered to fix data problems. Objects leave routine views through Withdrawal (restorable) rather than hard deletion, and historical duplicate identifiers go to a review queue instead of being renumbered automatically. This costs extra storage and review work, but the register is the evidence for institutional decisions.

Refined by ADR 0018 (MUS-794): staff can delete an Object's images, with approval when the Accessions approval workflow is on. Documents and images cited as evidence stay permanent.
