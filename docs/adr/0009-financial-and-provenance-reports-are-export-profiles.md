# Financial and provenance reports are export profiles with exact per-currency totals

The board valuation report, the insurer schedule and the provenance research report are profiles of the `register` export source (`valuation`, `insurance`, `provenance`), not reporting-module generators. They share the registers' durable job engine, typed column registry, criteria validation, manifest, permission rechecks and preview, and each profile carries its own version. A profile accepts only its own criteria, and a profile MuseumLite cannot produce stays unavailable rather than advertised.

Monetary totals are computed from exactly the rows written to the artifact, per group (inventory scope and purpose for valuations, coverage state for coverages) and per recognised ISO 4217 currency. They are exact decimal text, never added across currencies or converted, and committed with each page, so a retried page is never counted twice. Rows without a recognised currency are counted, not summed. The job stores the totals, and its status, manifest and completion audit entry publish them. There is no conversion rate in MuseumLite; a single figure across currencies is the reader's explicit choice, outside the report.

As-of follows the evidence. Immutable records (appraisals and their supersession, provenance claims, review decisions and evidence) reproduce exactly what was recorded at as-of. Validity and coverage state are judged at as-of from recorded dates. Mutable records with no history (coverage removal, policy cancellation or suspension, policy and coverage dates, object status, inventory scope, legacy provenance text) are captured when the report runs, and each profile's manifest says so. A historical insurer schedule is therefore an approximation for coverages removed or policies cancelled since, and we accept that rather than invent removal dates.
