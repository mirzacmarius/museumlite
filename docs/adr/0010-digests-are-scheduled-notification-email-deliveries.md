# Digests are notification email deliveries due at the recipient's local digest time

A daily or weekly digest is a delivery on the notification email ledger of ADR 0007: a `notificationEmailDeliveries` row of kind `digest` whose first attempt is due at the recipient's next digest time, with the notifications it carries listed in `notificationDigestItems`. When a recipient emails a category at a digest cadence, the dispatcher adds each new notification of that category to their waiting digest for the next digest time, starting the digest and scheduling its first attempt if none is waiting. There is one digest per recipient, Organization and digest time; a digest holds at most 100 notifications, and more start another digest for the same time so that every send reads a bounded list. The scheduled attempt sends the digest, and the five-minute sweep sends a digest whose run was missed, once and late. Nothing else is added: claim, attempts, backoff, the recipient's retry, the trace and 30-day retention are the instant email's, and the dispatcher stays the only entry point.

A digest time is a local time, plus a weekday for a weekly digest, in an IANA time zone saved from the recipient's browser. Times are computed with the calendar schedule shared with scheduled reports: a local time skipped by a daylight-saving change moves forward to the first valid time, and a repeated local time runs at its first occurrence. When the recipient changes their schedule, waiting digests move to the next time of the new schedule; when they switch to instant email or turn email off, waiting digests are due at once and the send decides what is still emailed. A run started before its digest is due belongs to a moved digest and does nothing.

Every attempt and every recipient retry checks the recipient again (membership, address and email transport), then each listed notification: it still exists, its category is still emailed to the recipient, and the recipient may still read the record it names. A notification that fails is left out of that attempt and records why, and a digest with nothing left is cancelled. The provider's idempotency key is `{delivery id}:{series}:{list}`, where `{list}` fingerprints the notifications the attempt lists: resending the same list is deduplicated by the provider, and a shorter list after an access change is a new email instead of one the provider refuses for a changed payload. If an attempt's result is lost and the list then shrinks, both versions can be delivered; we accept that over sending a notification to someone who may no longer read it.

Digest choices saved by the settings page used before MUS-658 have no time. They keep sending nothing until the recipient saves a time, and the settings page says so; they are not backfilled with a default time the recipient never chose.

Considered and rejected: a cron that scans every digest recipient each period, because its work grows with recipients instead of digests and it would be a second delivery path; digest periods as time windows instead of explicit lists, because a notification committed while a digest is assembled could fall between two windows; one digest per time regardless of size, because the send would read an unbounded list; and sending earlier digest choices at a default time, because that is not the schedule the recipient intended.
