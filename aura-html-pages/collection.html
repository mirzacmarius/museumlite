Curatorial Note

This life-size marble bust represents Marcus Aurelius, the philosopher-emperor who ruled the Roman Empire from 161 to 180 AD. The sculpture captures the emperor in his later years, characterized by a long, divided beard and heavy eyelids that convey a sense of weariness and stoic contemplation.

The bust wears a military cuirass and a paludamentum (general's cloak) pinned at the right shoulder with a circular fibula. The technical execution involves extensive drill work in the hair and beard, creating deep channels that play with light and shadow—a hallmark of Antonine portraiture.
